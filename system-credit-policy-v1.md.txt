# 1. Policy Principles

SYSTEM V1 follows these economic principles:

1. Training history is not automatically credit.
2. Credit is derived by the server evaluator.
3. The client cannot choose credit.
4. There is no writable XP balance.
5. There is no session-completion XP.
6. There is no achievement XP.
7. There is no PR XP.
8. `FAILED` sets receive `ZERO` credit in V1.
9. `PENDING` is economically inert.
10. Imported history cannot become official V1 capability evidence.
11. Manual credit is limited and cannot independently establish Rank.
12. Re-evaluation never creates net-positive credit merely because evaluator or policy metadata changed.
13. Credit is bounded by time, policy, provenance, and training-record identity.
14. All economic calculations use server-owned timestamps.

---

# 2. Canonical Credit Unit

The V1 ledger uses an abstract integer credit unit:

**CU = Credit Unit**

`CreditLedgerEntry.amount` is stored as an integer number of CU.

CU is an internal economic unit. It is not kilograms, calories, minutes, reps, or any physical measurement.

Physical training measurements remain canonical measurements defined by the Training Contract.

Credit-producing evaluator logic converts eligible training stimulus into CU according to the applicable policy.

## 2.1 Ledger amount

- Type: integer
- Minimum: `0`
- Maximum per ledger entry: `100 CU`
- Negative ledger entries are not permitted.
- Economic reversal uses `COMPENSATION`, not a negative amount.
- A compensation amount may offset a previous grant but may not itself create positive net credit.
